About Alpha Delta Studios

Alpha Delta Studios is a tiny independent studio making games, art, apps, and whatever other projects seem like a good idea at the time. Squirrel!

Games are the main focus right now. Some are small experiments, some begin as game jam projects, and some have a really bad habit of becoming much bigger ideas. Down the road, you'll probably find mobile apps (why has no one made ____), links to other art projects, or maybe something completely different. Who knows?

This site is where you'll find all of it. Current projects, finished releases, development updates, behind the scenes ramblings, and links to wherever you can actually play or find our stuff. Maybe I'll do a YouTube site too. Everyone says I'm supposed to.

We're small. We're figuring it out. We're trying to make cool shit.
