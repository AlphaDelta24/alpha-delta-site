Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Alpha Delta Studios is a small, independent studio. This policy is short because there isn't much to it — here's exactly what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

If you sign up for our mailing list, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Whether you've agreed to receive emails from us
  • The date you signed up

That's it. We don't track you around the site, and we don't collect anything beyond what you type into the signup form.

How we use it

We use your email address to send you updates when there's something worth sharing — a new game, a milestone, something real. We're not going to fill your inbox with filler.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data, to anyone, ever.
  • We don't share your data with other companies.
  • We don't use it for anything other than the emails described above.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email we send, or directly at our unsubscribe page. Once you unsubscribe, we stop emailing you — no further steps required on your end.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at contact@alphadeltastudios.com.
