Simple Reminders — Beta

Android Early Access — Alpha Delta Studios

Simple Reminders is a local-first Android reminder app. No account, no cloud, no subscription — your reminders live on your phone. I'm running a small private beta before launch and I'd love your help finding anything broken.

This is a beta build. It may have bugs. That's why you're here.

Before you install

Android doesn't allow installing apps from outside the Play Store by default. You'll need to allow it once — here's how:

  1. Download the APK file using the button above.
  2. Open your Downloads folder and tap the file.
  3. Android will ask if you want to allow installs from this source. Tap Settings, enable it, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Once installed you can turn that setting back off if you want.

This is standard procedure for beta testing before a Play Store release. The app is safe — it's built by me. No worries if you'd rather wait for the official release.

When you first open it

You'll be walked through a short setup — theme, notifications, and a couple of preferences. It takes about 30 seconds. After that you'll see a quick walkthrough of the app's features, then you're in.

When it asks for notification permission, grant it. That's the whole point of the app. Without it you'll see your reminders but they won't fire.

The setup will also ask you to disable "Pause app activity if unused" — do it. Android will put the app to sleep if you haven't opened it in a while, which blocks reminders. The setup walks you right to the setting.

What I'm testing

Just use it like a real reminder app for a few days. The things I most want to know:

You don't need to run structured tests. Just use it and tell me if something feels off.

Known issues

  • The title on the welcome screen animates slightly on load — cosmetic, being fixed.
  • Notifications do not yet reschedule after a phone reboot — this is in progress.

How to report bugs

The easiest way is right inside the app — Settings → Report a Bug. Give me a short description and steps to reproduce if you can. Screenshots are always helpful.

You can also report at alphadeltastudios.com/simple-reminders-bug.

A few things to know

  • Your data is stored locally on your phone. I don't have access to it.
  • Use Settings → Export to back up your reminders — good habit especially on a beta build.
  • If something goes seriously wrong, Settings → Reset App wipes everything and starts fresh.
  • Android only. iOS is not supported yet.
